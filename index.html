Surgical OR Book

An operative logbook for surgeons and surgical trainees.

Surgical OR Book keeps a personal record of the operations you take part in. Your logbook stays on your own phone. There is no account and no server, and the publisher never receives your cases.

Common questions

Where are my cases kept?

On your phone only. An automatic backup is saved in the app's own folder on the same phone. Export a backup from Profile and keep it somewhere else, so it survives a lost phone.

How do I move my logbook to a new phone?

On the old phone, go to Profile, Backup and security, and export a backup. On the new phone, install the app and import that file in the same place.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the app store, not in the app. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. After cancelling, the app keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, and the cases you have logged always remain available to view, edit and export.

I subscribed, but the app asks me to subscribe again.

Open the subscribe screen and tap Restore purchases. This checks with the app store and does not charge you.
