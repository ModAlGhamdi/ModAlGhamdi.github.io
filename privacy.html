Privacy notice

Surgical OR Book. Last updated 21 November 2026.

In short

The app works without an account. Your logbook stays on your phone. The publisher does not receive, see or store your cases. The app contains no advertising, tracking or analytics.

What the app stores

The cases you enter (date, procedure, your role, timings, patient age and sex, outcome and your notes), the names of supervising surgeons and hospitals you type, your profile, your saved lists and your settings. The app has no field for a patient name, date of birth or health card number, and you must not type them into any free-text field.

Hospital record numbers (MRN)

The MRN field is off until you turn it on. An MRN can identify a patient, so turn it on only where your hospital and the privacy law that applies to you allow it. When MRNs are stored, the app asks you to turn on the app lock and to protect exported backups with a password. You can turn the field off again in Profile; MRNs already saved stay on their cases until you delete them.

Where your data is kept

Your logbook is kept in the app's own storage on this phone. A backup copy is saved automatically in the app's folder on the phone. If you back up your phone, for example to iCloud or to a computer, the logbook is included in that backup under the terms of that service. If you turn on the iCloud Drive option, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account.

Voice logging

When you choose to speak a case, your voice is turned into text by speech recognition built into this phone. The audio is not sent anywhere and is not kept.

Sticker scanning

Scanning is off until you turn it on. When you scan, the photo of the sticker is read by text recognition built into this phone. The photo is not sent anywhere and is not kept. The app saves only the age, sex and hospital, the procedure if it is printed on the sticker and matches the procedure list, and the MRN if you turned that field on. The patient name is never saved.

Sign-off by QR code

A sign-off code holds the date, procedure and role of one case, the name in your profile, and the name the supervisor types. The codes pass directly from one screen to the other camera. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Phone features the app uses

The camera is used only when you scan a sticker or a sign-off code. The microphone and speech recognition are used only while you speak a case. Face ID, fingerprint or passcode checks are carried out by the phone; the app never receives your biometric data. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself. None of this needs an internet connection.

Exports and backups

PDF, Excel, CSV, backup and sync files, and the year-in-review picture, are created at your request and saved or shared where you choose. You are responsible for storing and sharing them safely. Backup, sync, CSV and Excel files include MRNs if you record them; the PDF logbook and the picture do not. An exported backup can be protected with a password. The sync file and the automatic backup kept on the phone are not password-protected.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by the app store. The publisher does not receive your name, your payment details or your store account. The app learns only whether a subscription is active, when it ends and whether it will renew.

Keeping your logbook safe

Use a passcode on your phone, so that the data stored on it is encrypted by the phone. You can also turn on the app lock in Profile, so that the logbook opens only for you.

Feedback

When you send feedback, the message contains only what you typed, the app version, the type of phone and the language. It is sent with the app you choose. Do not include patient details.

Your control

You can edit or delete any case, export everything, and delete your whole logbook from Profile at any time. Deleting all cases also removes the automatic backups on the phone. Deleting the app removes its data from the phone, but not files you exported or copies kept in iCloud. Because the publisher holds none of your data, there is nothing for the publisher to provide, correct or erase on request.

Age

The app is for medical professionals and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Your responsibilities

You remain responsible for following your hospital policy and the privacy law that applies to you whenever you record information about patient care.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when the app is updated. The current version is always available here. To ask a question about privacy, use Send feedback in Profile.
