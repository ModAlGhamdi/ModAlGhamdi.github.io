Purpose

Surgical OR Book is a personal record of operative experience for training, accreditation and professional development. It is not a medical record or a medical device, and must not be used to make clinical decisions or to care for patients.

Subscription

The app can be used free of charge for 48 hours from first use. After that, logging new cases requires a subscription for six months or one year. The price is shown in the app before you buy. Subscriptions are sold and billed by the app store, and renew automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You cancel in your store account settings, not in the app. After you cancel, the subscription stays active until the end of the period already paid for. Refunds are decided by the app store. One subscription covers your devices that use the same store account. Cases you have already logged remain available to view, edit and export without a subscription.

Who the app is for

The app is intended for surgeons and surgical trainees. It is not intended for patients or for the general public.

Accuracy

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you record and for confirming that reports meet the requirements of your program or regulator before you submit them.

Scanned, spoken and calculated values

Values filled in from a scanned sticker or from your voice, and figures calculated in reports, can be wrong. Check them before you save or rely on them.

Sign-off

A sign-off in the app records a name and a date. A sign-off by QR code also shows that a second phone confirmed the case, but not who was holding it. Neither is a verified electronic signature. Use the signature lines on the PDF logbook where a formal signature is required.

Your use of the app

Do not record patient names or other details that identify a patient, apart from an MRN where your hospital and the law allow it. Follow your hospital policy and the law that applies to you. Record only cases you took part in, and record them truthfully.

Backups

Keep your own backups. The automatic backup is stored on the same phone, so it is lost if the phone is lost or the app is deleted. Export a backup regularly from Profile and keep it somewhere else.
